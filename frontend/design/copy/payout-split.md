### Allocation Model Breakdown

#### 1. Smallholder Farmer Wallets (70%)
* **Role:** Direct pass-through dividend to individual producers.
* **Purpose:** Compensates smallholders for the labor and operational discipline of scheduled water drainage, canal gate management, and pump timing.
* **Settlement Rail:** Direct deposit into smallholder accounts at commercial banks or registered mobile money wallets via the Access Bank Sandbox (Simulated).
* **Sample Allocation:** `₦58,912,000 (est.) across active union cohort`

#### 2. Cooperative Development Fund (15%)
* **Role:** Capital investment and agronomic operational fund.
* **Purpose:** Managed by cooperative leadership to procure shared solar-powered irrigation pumps, high-yield certified FARO seed varieties, drainage canal maintenance, and local field agent training.
* **Settlement Rail:** Cooperative union institutional treasury account.
* **Sample Allocation:** `₦12,624,000 (est.)`

#### 3. MRV Platform & Verification Operations (15%)
* **Role:** Technical infrastructure and audit assembly.
* **Purpose:** Funds ongoing in-situ piezometer hardware maintenance, Sentinel-1 satellite imagery ingestion, tamper-evident cryptographic hash generation, and accredited VVB audit fees.
* **Settlement Rail:** CarbonTensor MRV operational pool.
* **Sample Allocation:** `₦12,624,000 (est.)`

### Accounting Invariant Note
Every NGN payout record links to its corresponding hectare boundary, sensor serial ID, and verified aeration duration. No intermediary broker deductions are permitted.
