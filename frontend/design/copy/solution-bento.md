CarbonTensor connects ground piezometer measurements, satellite radar cross-checks, and cooperative governance into an auditable data pipeline for carbon registries.

---

### Bento Card 1: In-Situ IoT Pani Pipe Telemetry (Large Surface)
* **Tag:** Ground Telemetry
* **Title:** Perforated sensor pipes track water depth every 6 hours.
* **Body:** Solar-powered pressure transducers inside perforated PVC field tubes log exact water table variations relative to the soil surface. Readings transmit via cellular telemetry, capturing natural percolation without requiring manual logbooks or invasive field audits.
* **Data Callout:** `Safe Aeration Threshold: -15.0 cm • Reading Cadence: 4x daily`

### Bento Card 2: Sentinel-1 SAR Radar Cross-Check (Medium Surface)
* **Tag:** Remote Sensing
* **Title:** Satellite radar verifies field inundation from orbit.
* **Body:** European Space Agency Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) passes provide independent surface backscatter observations. Comparing satellite moisture signals against ground sensor depth flags continuous submersion violations and tampering anomalies before audit submission.
* **Data Callout:** `12-day pass interval • Dual-polarization (VV/VH) moisture check`

### Bento Card 3: Cooperative Cohort Aggregation (Medium Surface)
* **Tag:** Union Governance
* **Title:** Aggregating 1-hectare parcels into registry-scale cohorts.
* **Body:** Individual smallholder plots in the Argungu cluster are mapped with GPS boundaries and registered to the cooperative union. Shared irrigation canals, synchronised drainage schedules, and unified roster records eliminate prohibitive individual audit fees.
* **Data Callout:** `Kebbi Union • 380 plots aggregated • 3,420 ha enrolled (est.)`

### Bento Card 4: Automated 70/15/15 Revenue Split (Wide Surface)
* **Tag:** Financial Settlement
* **Title:** Carbon revenues disburse directly to smallholder accounts.
* **Body:** Once independent verifiers approve the seasonal evidence dossier, proceeds flow into an audited escrow account. The contract automatically distributes 70% to verified farmer bank accounts, 15% to the cooperative development fund, and 15% to platform operations via the Access Bank Sandbox.
* **Data Callout:** `70% Farmer • 15% Cooperative • 15% MRV Platform`
